## Historical frame and fixed outcome

EN: In 381 a council at Constantinople reaffirmed Nicene faith. Its transmitted canons address religious teaching, territorial administration and the city’s honorary rank after Rome as New Rome. These provisions did not instantly end disagreement or create today’s denominations. Later letters and the surviving transmission of the associated creed require their own dates. Our local reconstruction separates three working contexts before the campaign offers a choice of historical perspective.

ES: En 381 un concilio de Constantinopla reafirmó la fe nicena. Sus cánones transmitidos tratan enseñanza religiosa, administración territorial y rango honorífico de la ciudad tras Roma como Nueva Roma. Esas disposiciones no terminaron de inmediato con los desacuerdos ni crearon las confesiones actuales. Las cartas posteriores y la transmisión conservada del credo asociado requieren sus propias fechas. Nuestra reconstrucción local separa tres contextos de trabajo antes de ofrecer una elección de perspectiva histórica.

EN: The three fictional local indexes and their distinct work shares are recorded. They do not reenact a council vote, prove universal reception or decide which later church is true. The Eagle and the Cross ends its selected frame in 381. Your chosen western or eastern perspective recommends an independent successor campaign. Both return to 337 and revisit part of this history through another narrator.
