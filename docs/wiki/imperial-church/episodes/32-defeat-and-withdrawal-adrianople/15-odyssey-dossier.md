## Historical frame and fixed outcome

EN: In 378 the Roman army suffered a major defeat near Hadrianopolis and Valens died. Ammianus gives alternative accounts of his death and describes scattered survivors. The exact battlefield remains disputed. This episode imagines one small withdrawal in the real regional landscape; it does not reconstruct an attested formation or reverse the defeat. The later siege of the city is a separate event.

ES: En 378 el ejército romano sufrió una gran derrota cerca de Adrianópolis y Valente murió. Amiano ofrece versiones alternativas de su muerte y describe supervivientes dispersos. La localización exacta de la batalla sigue discutida. Este episodio imagina una pequeña retirada en el paisaje regional real; no reconstruye una formación documentada ni invierte la derrota. El asedio posterior de la ciudad es un hecho distinto.

EN: The fictional foot and mounted groups completed their local withdrawal to a provisioned area outside the city. The Roman defeat and Valens’ death remain unchanged. No city gate, cottage or exact battlefield has been identified by this map. The next episode moves to Sirmium in 379 and the appointment of Theodosius; the local survivors do not cause that imperial decision.

ES: Los grupos ficticios de a pie y montados completaron su retirada local hasta una zona abastecida fuera de la ciudad. La derrota romana y la muerte de Valente permanecen. El mapa no identifica puerta urbana, casa ni campo de batalla exactos. El siguiente episodio pasa a Sirmio en 379 y al nombramiento de Teodosio; los supervivientes locales no causan esa decisión imperial.

EN: The six foot stragglers, four mounted scouts, fourteen supporting troops, local opponents, rally areas, payments, routes and boy’s story are invented. The scouts may fight using later-certified native proxies but are not historical cavalry units. Main originals have normal initial native health; no invented wound or healing bonus is silently simulated. There is no emperor rescue, corpse search, wagon-ring capture, mandatory sacrifice, religious guilt or guaranteed city admission. The survival thresholds are gameplay margins, not Ammianus’ army-wide casualty estimate.
