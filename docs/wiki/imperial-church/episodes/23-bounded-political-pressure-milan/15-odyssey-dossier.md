## Historical frame and fixed outcome

EN: Later church histories describe pressure at the Milan council of 355 to support the condemnation of Athanasius, followed by exile of dissenting bishops. The sources are partisan and do not supply a neutral transcript or a securely located meeting room. This local work preserves separate context notes; it neither casts the bishops as playable counters nor lets payment decide belief or exile.

ES: Las historias eclesiásticas posteriores describen presión en el concilio de Milán de 355 para apoyar la condena de Atanasio, seguida del destierro de obispos disidentes. Las fuentes son partidistas y no ofrecen una transcripción neutral ni una sala de reunión situada con seguridad. Este trabajo local conserva notas separadas de contexto; no convierte a los obispos en fichas jugables ni permite que un pago decida creencia o destierro.

EN: Separate fictional context copies and an ordinary outgoing work list reached different local stores. The council pressure and exiles remain historical narration; no goal acquits, condemns, restores or chooses a bishop. The next episode concerns the 356 nighttime assault at Alexandria, a different city and event.
