## Historical frame and fixed outcome

EN: In 362 Athanasius returned to Alexandria and later withdrew after Julian ordered him to leave. The emperor’s letter distinguishes return home from return to church; an anonymous Alexandrian chronicle reports local directions differently. These are interested accounts, not a neutral legal verdict. Julian’s death on the Persian expedition in 363 is a later, distant event. This local reconstruction follows fictional household work and separate working records, without making Athanasius a playable escort.

Son relatos interesados, no un veredicto jurídico neutral. La muerte de Juliano en la expedición persa de 363 es un hecho posterior y distante. Esta reconstrucción local sigue trabajo de hogares ficticios y registros separados, sin convertir a Atanasio en una escolta jugable.
