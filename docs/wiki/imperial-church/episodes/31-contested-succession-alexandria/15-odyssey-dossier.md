## Historical frame and fixed outcome

EN: Athanasius died in the period conventionally dated 373. An anonymous chronology names Peter as his successor; Socrates and Sozomen describe imperial intervention, Peter’s imprisonment and flight to Rome, and Lucius’ possession of the churches. These later Nicene accounts have their own commitments. Socrates’ consular synchronism conflicts with the adopted year. The playable withdrawal concerns ordinary fictional households, not Peter’s escape or a battle attested at these street corners.

Son relatos nicenos posteriores con compromisos propios. El sincronismo consular de Sócrates contradice el año adoptado. La retirada jugable afecta a hogares ficticios cotidianos, no a la fuga de Pedro ni a una batalla documentada en estas esquinas.

This local outcome changes no episcopal appointment or historical exile. Peter’s flight to Rome and Lucius’ possession are separately attributed reports.
