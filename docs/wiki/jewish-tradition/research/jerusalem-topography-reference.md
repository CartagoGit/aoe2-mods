# Jerusalem — reading terrain across dated sources

Research support for *Names beyond the Fallen House* (`01-a-city-of-names`). This page is not a first-century reconstruction, a completed dossier or an approved game map.

The [Wilson survey preview](../../../research/odyssey-research/images/jerusalem-wilson-1865-map-preview.jpg) shows the urban/topographic context surveyed in 1864–1865. Its [source, byte hash and inspection limits](../../../research/odyssey-research/jerusalem-wilson-1865-reference-collection.json) and [rights notice](../../../research/odyssey-research/images/jerusalem-wilson-1865-map-license.md) travel with the image. It is a provider-resized preview, not the full-resolution original. Modern-era buildings, wall outlines and streets remain dated to that survey; they cannot supply missing 70 CE footprints.

Three complementary evidence layers must remain distinct:

- The Wilson survey is useful for comparing broad slopes/valleys and later urban context; it is not georeferenced here and no street tracing is approved.
- The [IAA stepped-street excavation report](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=26274) distinguishes first-century material and destruction from later overbuilding. Its figures are linked references; no local image reuse licence is claimed.
- The [modern regional DSM record](../../../research/odyssey-research/jerusalem-modern-dsm-processing.json) describes a surface containing buildings and vegetation. It verifies a modern reference crop, not ancient ground or walkability.

The [local route/identity plan](../../../design/episode-plans/jewish-tradition/01-jerusalem70-route-and-identity-plan.md) supplies fictional activity controls within a real geographic frame. Before authoring terrain, root must determine which paths follow supported ground, where slopes/footprints require actual turns, and whether receiving courts can hold the original actors without collision. No direct edge in that graph becomes a certified ancient road. Valleys are not automatically rivers, and a later street does not establish an escape through the 70 siege.

The map download adds no new campaign identity, original actor, objective, canonical geography or full dossier. Historical/editorial review, actual terrain, engine orientation/pathfinding and difficulty calibration remain pending.
