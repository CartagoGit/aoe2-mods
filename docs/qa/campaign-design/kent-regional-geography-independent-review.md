## Findings

- **Modern controls and attribution are internally consistent.** The three features in `docs/research/odyssey-research/geography/kent-modern-place-controls.geojson` are OSM nodes 29038127/Reculver, 9827276975/Richborough, and 17916174/Canterbury. In each case GeoJSON order `[longitude, latitude]` exactly matches the corresponding provenance `[record.lon, record.lat]` and the candidate landmark. The candidate repeats the same three IDs, names, and coordinates. GeoJSON and provenance both identify OpenStreetMap contributors and ODbL 1.0; the response record also advertises ODbL. This supports use as current place-label orientation controls with attribution/license preserved, not as historical positions.
- **The provenance correction closes the raw-response reproducibility gap.** In `dbcba80`, each `records[]` entry now stores the complete captured HTTP response body as `rawResponseUtf8`, and `rawResponsePolicy` explains that live response metadata such as `generator` may vary. Re-encoding each stored string as UTF-8 reproduces all recorded byte counts and SHA-256 values exactly: Reculver 538 bytes / `a1b5ed1be3780f4987297f21200f2a16bdc104c00ed9e0aafd9cf60e90271c26`; Richborough 556 / `5a48f10af991c5e3f1f7e85c6a6c5056f6f21a2769a145e1c024a4b4000af696`; Canterbury 1,235 / `a56c60d851fde7051ac344140d0af6f8bea8ed5e54b4da1fb0833ae701502908`. Parsing each captured body yields an `elements[0]` object exactly equal to its provenance `record`; for all three, `[lon,lat]` equals the local GeoJSON coordinate and candidate landmark. The GeoJSON artifact remains 2,253 bytes with SHA-256 `f1d5bcae3325bad14800468c6b6ac0393536e6fccf5bc742562fbfd3e922934f`, unchanged from the first review. The previously observed differences in fresh live response hashes are consistent with variable server metadata and do not undermine reproducibility of the captured 2026-10-25 bodies. Attribution and ODbL fields are unchanged.
- **The 400-tile controls reproduce the existing helper.** `tools/design/geography.py::tile_point` maps all three candidate landmark coordinates against `profile.bbox=[1.04,51.25,1.45,51.41]` at size 400 to the exact stored tiles: Reculver `[158.52,131.74]`, Richborough `[269.22,247.52]`, Canterbury `[56.35,268.99]`. The helper uses north-up WGS84, cosine-corrected longitude and a square span padded by factor 1.12. The candidate labels its bbox an architect-selected viewing frame, stores derived `outerCellExtent`/`pointExtent`, and explicitly says it is not a published ancient extent. Neither the bbox nor those controls encode a landfall, shoreline, crossing, objective, or route.
- **The source cautions match the cited material.** Historic England, *Isle of Thanet Landscape Mapping Project* (Research Report 11/2025), printed pp. 4–5 describes the chalk ridge/scarp, plateau and dry valleys; printed p. 124 says the exact Augustine landing location is unknown and reports the Richborough identification as a later Thorne tradition. The English Heritage visitor history gives 597 arrival, a land grant east of Canterbury and probable 598 abbey foundation; the candidate distinguishes the mission from later abbey fabric and excludes the developed medieval precinct from the 597 map. These sources do not establish an exact arrival route or a 597 coast/water mask.

The arc's Gregory VI.51 note says it supports an underway mission/contact but does not establish the original dispatch date or precise first arrival; the brief states the same limit.

## Evidence and limits

The source report is [Historic England Research Report 11/2025](https://historicengland.org.uk/research/results/reports/9198/IsleofThanetLandscapeMappingProject_AerialInvestigationandMapping); see its printed pp. 4–5 and 123–124. English Heritage's [history of St Augustine's Abbey](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/st-augustines-abbey/history-and-stories/history/) is a visitor summary, not a location survey. OSM attribution/license terms are linked from the provenance record at `https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright` and `https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/`.

Checks were limited to source-file inspection, fresh API responses recorded in the prior review, UTF-8 byte/hash and parsed-record comparisons against the corrected captured bodies, local coordinate comparisons, the existing `tile_point` helper and targeted registry/count/link checks. No project test suite, renderer, scenario validator, game, historical approval, or production gate was run. The candidate still needs independent resolution of dated shoreline/channel evidence and final activity-map geography before canonical promotion or gameplay design.
