# Manifest-backed water journeys: architect contract

Design owner: gpt-6. Status: design-only, unimplemented, independent review pending. Used by imperial display orders8–9; historical authoring remains blocked. This contract extends the design dossiers only. It is not an accepted `capabilityProfile` predicate or a native implementation.

Each passenger is an original identity, including loadable cargo proxies. Funding labels do not claim a carried food/wood inventory. Native cargo proxies must be loadable units, physically movable, separately identified and source-qualified; scenery cannot stand for cargo. Two original carriers have four scenario slots each, shared across every passenger requirement. Native capacity restriction and eligible boarding must be demonstrated against the pinned game/parser; if this cannot be enforced, the architect revises the contract before content authoring. MiniMax does not change capacity by guesswork.

The completed readiness objective, including funding, latches its actual receipted identities. Only those originals may begin a qualifying journey. Pay readiness last: ten staged auxiliaries permit two later losses, while eight staged auxiliaries permit none. A live unstaged original cannot replace a dead eligible passenger. After the crossing latch, inland survival uses the exact crossed ledger. An irreversible eligibility deficit defeats the main mission immediately; the broad initial ten-person list cannot hide it.

A qualifying per-passenger journey records, in order: complete readiness receipt, observed boarding with capacity reservation, departure from the origin shore, real water transit, destination approach, and observed disembarkation of the same passenger from the same carrier. One journey may use either authored water corridor. Positions are observations of a real native map; district centres/straight SVG connectors are not a navigation proof. A opposite-bank arrival, generated flag, elapsed timer, empty returning ship or another passenger’s manifest cannot substitute. Completed passenger IDs and route receipts survive save/load and count once.

One authoritative carrier manifest serves auxiliaries and all cargo requirements. Boarding reserves a slot atomically; disembarkation frees it; departure/unload/loss cannot race into duplicate identities or capacity. Repeated trips and load schedules are allowed. A destroyed carrier irreversibly loses every passenger actually aboard at the observed loss; already unloaded passengers survive independently. If one carrier is lost empty, the other original carrier must finish all remaining trips without side rewards. If both are lost before every required crossing is complete, the mission is irrecoverable; losing them after all required crossings does not retroactively undo safe passengers.

The original auxiliaries, carriers and loads never respawn. Military replacement budget in order8 applies only to ordinary screen troops through its separate pending capability. All six optional tasks remain on the preparation bank with their own identities and finite rewards; none expands ship capacity, fabricates main cargo or unlocks the only crossing. The river convoy’s longer eastern land detour must remain outside the finite hostile pocket in all difficulties. The two-shore mission has no hostile fleet or hidden deadline.

Implementation responsibility stays with the architect in i00003: S1 typed actions/state/validators/fingerprint migration and adversarial reference fixtures; S2 dated shore geometry, water spawn/boarding/unloading footprints, both water corridors and land paths; S3 native manifests, loadable proxies, observer/event order, loss/save persistence and export; s00001 actual game evidence. These are extensions required before the affected f00003/S14 and S15 content slices can start. No proposal status or peer approval is changed by this document.

Required adversarial evidence: five boarding attempts on one four-slot carrier across different requirements; wrong passenger/carrier, premature readiness or prior buffered arrival; double boarding/departure/unload, repeated trip and save/load at every event; occupied versus empty ship loss; last ship lost before versus after required crossings; eight staged plus one loss versus ten staged plus two losses; unstaged and uncrossed replacements rejected; one-ship skip-all completion at each difficulty; optional loss/no reward after terminal; real water/land path, ancient-shore uncertainty and measured durations.
