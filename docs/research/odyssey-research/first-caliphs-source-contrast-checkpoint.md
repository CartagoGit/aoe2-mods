## Findings that require explicit author decisions

| Scope | Contrast and required treatment |
|---|---|
| Medina 644, episode 26 | The [chronology](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chronology-of-iranian-history-part-1/?generate_pdf=1), PDF p.14 row 644, names Hormozan as Umar’s killer. [Shahbazi’s dedicated article](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hormozan/?generate_pdf=1), PDF p.2, distinguishes Abu Lo’lo’s assassination from Ubayd Allah’s subsequent accusation and killing of Hormozan. Do not copy the chronology’s conflation or convert an accusation into a proven conspiracy. The canonical candidate itself names no assassin and is retained. |
| Antioch 638, episode 23 | The relevant [Kaegi passage](https://almuslih.org/wp-content/uploads/Library/Kaegi%2C%20W%20-%20Byzantium.pdf) is printed p.146, PDF p.159, with note 84 acknowledging disputed chronology. The archived locator printed p.158 points to a different discussion. The located passage describes occupation without further armed resistance; it cannot justify an invented Antioch siege or prove unanimous consent. |
| Damascus/Jerusalem, episodes 18/22 | Kaegi’s tentative sequence differs from the chronology’s 635 Damascus row and from [Schick’s summary](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/christian-communities-of-palestine-from-byzantine-to-islamic-rule/islamic-conquest-the-mid630s-to-640/75A757E519D6EF48BD198F3BDBAFADC3), which qualifies Jerusalem 638. Preserve the alternatives; a year table does not establish surrender clauses. |
| Alexandria, episode 25 | The [Fordham compilation](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/source/642Egypt-conq2.asp) includes more than the initial conquest. Its al-Baladhuri selection explicitly introduces a subsequent Byzantine return in year 25. Keep that phase and its violence separate from a 641/642 scenario. The ecclesiastical account and conquest account remain different retrospective traditions, not a harmonized script. |
| Cyprus/Phoenix, episodes 27/29 | [O’Sullivan’s public notes](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/byzantine-and-modern-greek-studies/article/abs/sebeos-account-of-an-arab-attack-on-constantinople-in-654/B067CB79A2ADF5B9A47165D72E00737D), 15–16 and 40–42, discuss chronology and proposed reconstructions. Stratos objects to fleet estimates; O’Sullivan considers them plausible within his hypothesis. The archived wording must not collapse these positions or turn the numbers into certified scenario forces. No full article, inscription edition or chronicles were read here. |
| Hudaybiyya, episode 11 | The [TDV entry](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/hudeybiye-antlasmasi) supplies location/agreement context, but also makes prejudicial generalizations about Jews and tribes. Reject those as objective facts, narrator authority or behavioral rules for a population. The article is not the original treaty. |
| Qadesiya, episode 20 | [Lewental’s source criticism](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/qadesiya-battle/) preserves competing chronologies and describes narrative embellishments. A convenient modern date, storm or heroic anecdote must not become mandatory factual mechanics. |
| Ctesiphon, episode 21 | [Kröger’s entry](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ctesiphon/?generate_pdf=1) has a conquest date and selected topographical cautions; those do not certify a precise Tigris crossing or resolve the wider campaign chronology. |
