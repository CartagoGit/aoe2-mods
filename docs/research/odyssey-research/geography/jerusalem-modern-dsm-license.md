# Jerusalem modern DSM regional reference

This file documents the source and notices for `jerusalem-modern-dsm-regional-crop.tif` and its derived scientific preview. The TIFF is a regional integer-window crop of one public Copernicus GLO-30 COG tile. Copernicus describes GLO-30 as a 30 m digital surface model (DSM) of the reflective surface, including buildings, infrastructure and vegetation. It is not bare-earth terrain, an ancient surface, archaeological footprints, historical placement evidence, or a playable-map decision. Its inclusion does not authorize a historical reconstruction or game use.

Source tile: [public GLO-30 AWS COG](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N31_00_E035_00_DEM/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N31_00_E035_00_DEM.tif). Product description: [Copernicus DEM](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM). The [AWS mirror readme](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/readme.html) states that GLO-30 Public tiles are provided free to the general public under the linked licence and are COGs arranged on a one-degree grid. The [Copernicus WorldDEM-30 licence (PDF)](https://docs.sentinel-hub.com/api/latest/static/files/data/dem/resources/license/License-COPDEM-30.pdf) grants reproduction, distribution, communication to the general public, adaptation, modification and combination without charge, subject to its obligations. This is the GLO-30 Public product; it is not the separately licensed WorldDEM-10 product.

The processing record gives the requested bbox, source and crop hashes, HTTP headers, exact integer source window, CRS/GeoTIFF interpretation, affine transform, sample-centre and outer-cell extents, tools, and sample-equality verification. The preview is a display rendering of the same unmodified crop values with a 2nd–98th percentile contrast stretch; it does not create new terrain values.
