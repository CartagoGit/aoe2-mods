# Edirne modern surface-height research reference

Applies to `edirne-modern-dsm-regional-crop.tif` and `../images/edirne-modern-dsm-regional-preview.png`. These are a lossless regional crop and a labelled visualization of Copernicus GLO-30 public data, not a reconstruction of378. Preview colors represent surface height, including low dry land; blue is not a water or flood mask. No battlefield, ancient road or military position is identified.
