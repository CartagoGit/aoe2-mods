# Hijaz modern DSM research references

Applies to the four `*-modern-dsm-regional-crop.tif` and corresponding scientific previews for badr, uhud, hudaybiyya and mina-aqaba. Three public GLO-30 tiles were downloaded; only lossless integer crops and height previews are retained in the project. No source sample is resampled or edited in the TIFF crops. Processing and complete padded-square coverage are recorded in [processing](../hijaz-modern-dsm-processing.json).

These are modern surface heights, potentially including buildings/infrastructure/vegetation. They are not ancient ground, camp/well/building footprints, permanent water, terrain/passability or game verification. Public visitor/admin markers identify contemporary reference points only. No programme endorsement or campaign-use approval is implied.
