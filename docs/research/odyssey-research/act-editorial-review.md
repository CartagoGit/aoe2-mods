# Editorial direction for the 45 odyssey acts

Drafted 23 October; reviewed 24 October 2026 · architect `gpt-6` · partial d00002/S1 component.

The [authored research data](act-editorial-review.json) bind entry, structural pivot and exit to semantic episode IDs for all 45 acts. A pivot identifies a change of player problem or acute pressure; it is not necessarily a battle, a historical turning point proven by a source, or the hardest measured mission. These cards direct the architect’s remaining dossiers and do not authorize MiniMax to invent their objectives.

This is a dated design assessment. Its source hashes identify the candidate examined; they are not a live approval system or a substitute for DelendAI. Historical source review, complete individual dossiers, ancient terrain, native support and played calibration remain separate.

## Resolved heading discrepancies

Eight nominal year bands are widened to include already selected episodes or their stated alternatives. No episode date, source qualification, identity, ordering, objective, screen, narrator or gameplay contract changes. Headings describe editorial frames; an earlier document recalled in a later local interlude is not newly dated to that interlude. Archaeological ranges and a law’s later effective date remain source qualifications, not automatic bounds for an encounter.

## Shared difficulty, narration and continuity requirements

Military multipliers apply only to an authored military problem. They cannot add enemies to a peaceful event, increase native unit speed/damage, or turn a local success into a different historical outcome. Act intensity must vary: aftermath, reception and interpretation are legitimate changes of pace. None of this is a measured easy/standard/hard balance curve.

Each complete dossier still needs finite budgets, quantities, routes, protected recovery and its own warning/failure/reentry conditions for all three difficulties. Main victory must remain feasible with all 6–8 secondaries skipped; their finite rewards, at least four mechanics and three combinable approaches cannot replace the main task or create one mandatory playstyle. The 63 drafts and 54 static risk candidates remain unchanged by these metadata corrections.

A second letter trip does not prove a distinct mission. Before accepting adjacent correspondence/archive episodes, the architect must demonstrate different actors, original objects, initial world state, route decisions, competing constraints and recovery. If those cannot be specified coherently, the campaign design remains unready; MiniMax does not pad it or decide the repair.

The common narrator gives a sober, attributed date/place/event summary, then explicitly yields to the campaign persona. Private motives, inherited documents, disputed reports and eyewitness claims stay distinct. The same persona persists throughout its campaign within its lifespan and knowledge. Long chronological gaps require an explicit transition rather than invented filler or a new witness memory.

Only the actual final selected episode completes the odyssey or recommends successors. Predecessor recap/warning remains nonblocking and campaign progress remains cumulative. Rewinding or overlapping timelines is stated explicitly, including 313 to 312 and 381 back to 337 perspectives. The two Islamic successors of first-fitna are perspectives the player may choose, not moral verdicts.
