## Purpose and exact ownership

Produce private artificial editor diagnostics at 300×300 and 400×400 through the existing exporter. This lets a tester observe loading, coordinate orientation and ordinary movement at the intended map scale while historical planning continues. It is a sparse technical fixture, not a performance proof at a campaign's population/trigger caps, real geography, a historical map, a DEM conversion or a playable campaign release.
