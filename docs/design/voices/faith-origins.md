### backstory

EN: Raised in a Caesarean household of copyists, she learned to compare inherited writings with their omissions. An accidental loss of work pages in her own adulthood made her wary of confident reconstructions. A fictional working stay in Milan near 313 gives the last episode a local personal frame. Her earlier stories are inherited, not remembered.

Una estancia laboral ficticia en Milán cerca de 313 sitúa personalmente el último episodio. Las historias anteriores son heredadas, no recuerdos.
