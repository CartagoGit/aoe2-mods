# Farid, the Field Medic / Farid, el sanitario de campaña

The Shattered Oath · `farid-field-medic` · c. 624–690 CE

Expanded odyssey voice bible. Architect-authored fiction; independent editorial, native and audio review pending. This biography is not an ancient source or evidence of a historical eyewitness. It does not approve any scenario or portrait.

## Common narrator and handover

EN: Sober objective source-qualified opening, then handover to campaign persona

ES: Apertura sobria y objetiva con límites de fuente, seguida del paso a la persona de campaña

factual-date-place-event → relevant-evidence-uncertainty → handover → campaign-personal-interpretation

No confessional verdict, miracles as attributed belief, motives distinguished from reported actions, strongest supported account given without manufacturing false equivalence.

Common narrator can explain a modern excavation or document catalogue; contemporaneous fictional persona cannot pretend to have witnessed it.

## Personal history and delivery

### profession

EN: Field attendant in fictional Iraqi relief networks

ES: Auxiliar sanitario de redes ficticias de auxilio iraquí

### temperament

EN: Tender, terse under pressure, angry at easy verdicts; grief makes him cautious

ES: Tierno, lacónico bajo presión y airado ante juicios fáciles; el dolor le vuelve prudente

### backstory

EN: He works near Iraqi camps and hears incompatible explanations from people needing the same care. Fictional road transfers place him near local relief tasks, never at the center of private diplomacy. His skill cannot save every person; his narration cannot decide every disputed motive.

ES: Trabaja cerca de campamentos iraquíes y oye explicaciones incompatibles de personas que necesitan el mismo cuidado. Traslados ficticios lo sitúan junto a tareas locales de auxilio, nunca en el centro de diplomacia privada. Su oficio no puede salvar a todos; su narración no puede decidir todos los motivos disputados.

### spokenRegister

EN: Short grounded observations; does not turn every wound into a metaphor

ES: Observaciones breves y concretas; no convierte cada herida en metáfora

### blindSpot

EN: He wants suffering to disprove every cause; learns care is not a historical verdict

ES: Quiere que el sufrimiento refute toda causa; aprende que cuidar no equivale a emitir un veredicto histórico

### refrain

EN: Care is something I can offer; a verdict is harder.

ES: Puedo ofrecer cuidados; un veredicto es más difícil.

### samplePersonalVoice

EN: I knew which bowl was empty. I did not know which account of the negotiation would survive.

ES: Sabía qué cuenco estaba vacío. No sabía qué relato de la negociación sobreviviría.

The sample voice is invented prose, never an authentic historical quotation.
