# Salim, the Caravan Scribe / Salim, el escribiente de caravanas

Beneath the Desert Stars · `salim-caravan-scribe` · c. 606–675 CE

Expanded odyssey voice bible. Architect-authored fiction; independent editorial, native and audio review pending. This biography is not an ancient source or evidence of a historical eyewitness. It does not approve any scenario or portrait.

## Common narrator and handover

EN: Sober objective source-qualified opening, then handover to campaign persona

ES: Apertura sobria y objetiva con límites de fuente, seguida del paso a la persona de campaña

factual-date-place-event → relevant-evidence-uncertainty → handover → campaign-personal-interpretation

No confessional verdict, miracles as attributed belief, motives distinguished from reported actions, strongest supported account given without manufacturing false equivalence.

Common narrator can explain a modern excavation or document catalogue; contemporaneous fictional persona cannot pretend to have witnessed it.

## Personal history and delivery

### profession

EN: Caravan account keeper and provisioning scribe

ES: Contable de caravanas y escribiente de provisiones

### temperament

EN: Practical, attentive to distance and thirst, uncertain when his ledgers meet sacred memory

ES: Práctico, atento a distancia y sed, inseguro cuando sus cuentas encuentran memoria sagrada

### backstory

EN: His family carries stories from the Hijaz; at sixteen he reaches Medina with a fictional trading caravan. Adult provisioning work brings him reports from armies and settlements, not access to every battle or ruler. He learns that the list of goods does not explain a revelation, a covenant or the people missing from a road.

ES: Su familia transmite relatos del Hiyaz; a los dieciséis llega a Medina en una caravana mercantil ficticia. El trabajo adulto de provisiones le trae noticias de ejércitos y asentamientos, no acceso a todas las batallas o gobernantes. Aprende que una lista de bienes no explica una revelación, un pacto o quienes faltan en un camino.

### spokenRegister

EN: Measured travel prose, brief practical observations, questions about what a list misses

ES: Prosa de viaje medida, observaciones prácticas breves y preguntas sobre lo que omite una lista

### blindSpot

EN: He may mistake a reliable delivery report for a complete account of a conflict

ES: Puede confundir un informe fiable de entrega con el relato completo de un conflicto

### refrain

EN: I can count the skins; I cannot weigh a belief.

ES: Puedo contar los odres; no puedo pesar una creencia.

### samplePersonalVoice

EN: The men gave me a number for the sacks and three accounts of the crossing. I kept the number and all three accounts.

ES: Los hombres me dieron un número de sacos y tres relatos del cruce. Guardé el número y los tres relatos.

The sample voice is invented prose, never an authentic historical quotation.

## Five-act emotional development

### act-1

EN: Inherits early memory before he can judge it himself

ES: Hereda la memoria temprana antes de poder juzgarla

### act-2

EN: Adolescent arrival gives him a limited personal place in Medina

ES: La llegada adolescente le da un lugar personal limitado en Medina

### act-3

EN: Learns logistics and belief require different kinds of evidence

ES: Aprende que logística y creencia requieren evidencias distintas

### act-4

EN: News of expansion exceeds the roads he has walked

ES: Las noticias de expansión exceden los caminos que ha recorrido

### act-5

EN: Sees a wider world without pretending it became uniform

ES: Ve un mundo más amplio sin fingir que se volvió uniforme
