# Historical odyssey selections

273 selected episodes across nine campaigns. This is an editorial selection, not273completed dossiers or playable maps. The approved baseline catalogue still contains39historical missions. Source checks, complete objectives, map footprints, all player-facing text and native difficulty calibration remain separate acceptance gates.

Each campaign has five acts and its own situated fictional narrator. The shared factual narrator opens and closes each episode before yielding to that person. English titles are the source; Spanish titles are supplied. The original raw corpus preserves research provenance even when an architect correction supersedes a candidate.

Optional loss cannot defeat a main objective; rewards require unique delivery receipts, finite reserved stock and atomic persistent completion. Main success must remain feasible when all optional tasks are skipped.
