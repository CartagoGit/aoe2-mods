## Pressure must create a choice

There is no main payment, production objective, timer for the historical fire or requirement to destroy the opposition. The main problem is moving distinct vulnerable originals through disclosed local pressure while retrieving all three fixed bearers. A route can be longer and safer without being a historical escape. The same household loss allowance and bearer rule apply at every difficulty.

Two proposed pressure origins already have separate controls: western pocket at (71.71, 122.07), eastern pocket at (350.52, 153.95). The western household control is (133.67, 131.18), about 62.63 tiles from its pocket; the eastern household control is (296.31, 149.40), about 54.40 tiles from its pocket. Those are straight control separations, not activation lead times, weapon ranges or certified terrain. Neither origin is a receiving court or a licence to spawn enemies among civilians. Pending pathfinding must establish a real approach and enough visible reaction room from each original spawn including offsets.

A permanent stationary guard at each pocket would not implement the intended pressure. An unrestricted enemy hunting any civilian across the entire map would invalidate the disclosed receiving/recovery plan. Root chooses finite local acquisition, pursuit and return, with original actors present from scenario start, ordinary engine combat and no replenishment. Before native materialization, i00003/S3 must implement and test those bounds, including target loss, projectile behaviour, kiting across a boundary and save/load with a retained target. It already records that missing reusable operation; a drawn polygon is not working AI. Do not replace it with a timer, global hunt or a frozen enemy.

The local stories do not establish a named Roman detachment, battle order, historical weapons roster or universally hostile religious population. Opposing actors are fictional armed participants in the siege context; label their gameplay role, not a whole people or faith as the enemy. Main households and record bearers are never recruited into a religious army. No controllable holy figure, conversion or ransom is part of the solution.

## Difficulty rules selected by the architect

Difficulty changes exposure and escort allocation, rather than making optional tasks mandatory or changing unit statistics. The numerical roster remains blocked on an actual footprint, acquisition and route design. A mechanical author must not fill that gap with familiar 72/96 enemy caps or three arbitrary numbers.

| Profile | Required decision | Allowed mistake/recovery | Must be disproved before accepting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | Consolidate one escort, approach one retrieval branch at a time and regroup at disclosed junctions. | Withdraw with the living originals before the irreversible threshold; the same two-household total allowance remains. | Immediate acquisition of an original at spawn, compulsory sacrifice, or a required split that the briefing did not teach. |
| Standard | Choose between a slower consolidated retrieval and parallel coverage of the two local approaches. | Transfer an existing escort between junctions or reroute living originals; no free replacement appears. | All pressure is avoidable by an empty central walk, or one group can clear every encounter without exposure/attention tradeoff. |
| Hard | Coordinate cover and retrieval around both pressure regions, using the same original identities and ordinary stats. | Give up an optional detour or regroup before committing a vulnerable bearer; no narrow mandatory input window. | Only one exact build/order works, all optional rewards are needed, the first tolerated loss already makes completion impossible, or a consolidated alternative is advertised but cannot succeed. |

A fixed pressure zone may be solved by a large escort in sequence; with unlimited time that could dominate every profile. Conversely, two permanently active pursuers could make unattended original households die before any reasonable approach. Both are explicit adverse cases, not tuning details to defer to MiniMax. Decide actual acquisition predicates and reachable recovery pockets first; then choose exact finite forces and test both advertised portfolios. Do not add a countdown merely to hide either defect. A failure of both portfolios returns this design to root, rather than forcing a historical licence or weakening the six-of-eight rule.

## Finite funding, without fictitious economic difficulty

Root selects an escort/retrieval episode with no main resource payment, trainable replacements, food/gold reward sink or regenerative income. The only current resource expenditure is the optional store operation: a fixed 50 wood payment plus actual restoration of its original damaged object. `resourceReward: {}` is now supported by accepted i00009/S2 (product `0cf4d064`, verdict `9f4be255`). Optional aid/neighbor delivery restores no unit and grants no resource bonus; their local outcomes are stated explicitly.

The legacy projection can express this documentary envelope with authored starting wood 60, discretionary wood 69, and zero main cost, protected recovery stock, replacement budget and reward reserve. Its shared multipliers derive the following totals. The “bank” is accounting only until an actual finite location and collection operation is authored; it must never become an invisible free credit.

| Profile | Starting wood | Derived finite bank allowance | Accessible without any secondary | After the sole 50-wood optional operation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Easy | 69 | 0 | 69 | 19 |
| Standard | 60 | 9 | 69 | 19 |
| Hard | 51 | 18 | 69 | 19 |

Even the initial hard stock covers the optional operation. The main route spends zero and remains financially possible when every side is skipped. The equal accessible total is deliberate reserve accounting, not proof of equal tactical difficulty or a difficulty lever. Food, gold and stone are zero because no approved operation uses them. No gathering trip is required to make the repair possible. If those small optional banks are represented later, place them finitely outside required receiving footprints and document their real use; otherwise choose a supported explicit no-bank profile in a separately scoped architecture change, not a silent cache.

There is no resource-funded recovery in this design: recovery means regrouping/rerouting eligible living originals. A generic help template that suggests nonzero recovery supplies or reward stock would be misleading. The current factory's fixed `economy-help` says those accounts are reserved without describing that they are empty; native presentation must use the actual episode amounts and explicitly identify the optional repair, rather than promise nonexistent supplies. Record this within i00003/S4 presentation requirements; this partial design does not certify the template for release.
