# Copernicus DSM sample fixture

Applies to `edirne-dsm-sample-grid.json`: 25 contiguous samples copied from the project's verified modern Edirne DSM crop, encoded as an internal JSON sample grid. No resampling was applied to these 25 values. Source window, georeference, original/crop hash, collector and architect normalization are recorded in the JSON. It is a technical fixture, not an ancient surface, DTM, playable map or archaeological location.
